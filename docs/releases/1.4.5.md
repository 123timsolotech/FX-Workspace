# FX Workspace 1.4.5

Faster big models with one button, and pictures you can make wider or taller - more of the scene, with the original kept exactly.

**Updating:** from 1.4.2 or later, Settings › About › **Check for updates** › **Update now**. From older versions, run `FX-Workspace-Setup-1.4.5.exe` over them. Your chats, settings, keys and models stay as they are.

## Faster big models: Tune speed

- **Settings › Models › Tune speed:** pick a model and press the button. It tries bigger batches for reading your prompt, and for mixture-of-experts models (like Ornith 35B-A3B) how many expert layers stay on the CPU, and measures each one on this PC.
- **It keeps a setting only if it's clearly faster:** reading the prompt 10% faster without writing getting slower, or writing 5% faster. A split that spills into shared GPU memory is never kept, however fast it looks.
- It shows what it tried and what it kept, with the numbers. If anything fails along the way, the model's settings go back exactly as they were.

## Expand a picture

- **Images › a picture › Expand…:** choose the sides (all, left and right, top and bottom, or one side) and how much more (25%, 50% or 100%). Optionally say what the new area should show; otherwise it uses the picture's own prompt.
- The picture's own model draws the new area. **The original's pixels stay exactly as they were**, and the colours are matched at the join so there's no visible seam.
- The result is a new picture next to the original; the original stays. Up to 1536 pixels on the long side.
