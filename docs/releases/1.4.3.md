# FX Workspace 1.4.3

A visual release: a new thinking mark that moves with the work, every appearance checked screen by screen (the Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Gemini looks are out of Beta), a short welcome for new installs, and a calmer Settings.

**Updating:** from 1.4.2, Settings › About › **Check for updates** › **Update now**. From older versions, run `FX-Workspace-Setup-1.4.3.exe` over them. Your chats, settings, keys and models stay as they are. After the update the app shows what changed, once.

## Orbs: a thinking mark that moves with the work

- **Three orbs** - a core and two satellites - show what it's doing: they orbit while it thinks, sweep along a line while it reads, swing out like radar while it searches, click round like gears for a tool, trail like a pen while it writes, swirl colours while it draws, and rest while it waits for you.
- **No cuts between them.** When the work changes, the orbs fly from where they are into the new pattern; a quick run of changes never makes them jump. Research's progress bar and the mini window's chat strip use the same mark, and it carries on when the Thinking row hands over to the line under it.
- **Dressed for each appearance:** ink dots or warm planets with a faint ring in Classic, soft suns in Sunrise, square pixels that step in Terminal, moons over a corona in Eclipse, glowing orbs with light trails in Aurora, a clay asterisk in Claude, one dot that opens up in ChatGPT, hairline rings in Grok, gradient orbs and a sparkle in Gemini.
- **Light on the PC:** one drawing loop for every mark at 30 frames a second, asleep when none is on screen and while the window is hidden in the tray - about a third of what the old marks cost the page (measured: 2.8% against 8.5% of a core, the orbs' own code 0.6%). Reduced motion and Motion off show a still shape for each kind of work.
- **Settings › Appearance › Thinking mark:** Auto (orbs; Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Gemini keep their own marks), Orbs everywhere, or Legacy - each appearance's mark from before. A preview walks through all seven.

## Every appearance, checked

- **A new check** goes through every screen in every appearance, light and dark - 486 screens, from the chat and its approval cards to every Settings page, Images, Labs, the mini window and a phone-width window - and measures each text against what's behind it, every keyboard focus ring, colours borrowed from the wrong appearance, and anything cut off or scrolling sideways.
- **What it found is fixed**, in all nine appearances:
  - secondary text was too faint in places (the model line in the sidebar, the credit line, key hints, Settings' palette) - it now always reads at the accessibility standard's 4.5:1, whatever the appearance or accent;
  - colour on coloured pills and warnings (Types or clicks, Changes things, This cannot be undone, Trained, the diff in an approval) reads clearly;
  - the "Types or clicks" pill was the size of a text box;
  - the sidebar's resize handle showed no focus ring;
  - icons and times sat glued to their labels on Images, Videos and Music ("☆Favourites", "Fast~13 s").
- **Switching appearances** was checked from every one of the 18 appearance/scheme pairs to every other: nothing of the old one stays behind.
- **The Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Gemini appearances are out of Beta.**

## A welcome for new installs

A new install opens on three short steps, each one skippable: **a chat model** that suits this PC (it reads your graphics card and memory and offers Qwen3 8B, 4B or 1.7B - one click downloads it, checked against its published SHA-256; nothing goes to the network before that click), **a look**, and **what stays on this PC**. Settings › About › Show the welcome again brings it back.

## Settings, calmer

- **Search goes to the setting.** A search result opens the part of the page it's on, scrolls to the setting and lights it up for a moment.
- **Connections** now holds Connectors, MCP servers and Channels in one place, and **Web search and network** holds both halves of how traffic leaves this PC - three fewer stops in Settings. Every old link (the network indicator, "Manage" on a connector) still lands on the right section.
- **Notifications held back by Windows:** when Focus assist (Do not disturb) or a full-screen game is holding pop-ups back, Settings › Appearance › Notifications says so, with a button to Windows' page.
- **Model use outside your replies** (Settings › This PC) folds repeats into one line ("×12") with their tokens and time added up, and shows what the "Only for my replies" switch has stopped.

## Fixes

- **The app's server could stop under a running window.** It checks every second that the desktop app is still open; one check that failed while the PC was busy (loading a very large model) read as "closed". It now takes three checks in a row, and a check that can't tell counts as open.
- **The NCM story writer** (Labs, Beta) stopped writing with HazyResearch's current code ("generate(idx=None) requires..."). It now starts each story the way their own scripts do - a TinyStories story in about 3 seconds, level by level.
- Right after the app started, its hidden approval window could take the keyboard focus for a moment. It is now made only when an approval actually needs it.
- Phone access says plainly that "Add to Home screen" makes a shortcut (a full app install needs HTTPS).

## Known issues

- **Discord and Slack** stay Beta: they're still tested only against recorded messages, not a real bot.
- **The MoE cache test** shows llama-server's raw error when the model can't load (for example when its context doesn't fit in the graphics memory). Lowering the model's context in Settings › Models lets it load.
